Vega change process

Project Vega (VEG-390) · Owner: Rhea Vance, Tech Lead · Folder: /Vega/Support/Changes · Verified 30 Sep 2026

Types of change

TypeExampleApproval
StandardAdding a user to a known groupPre-approved, log it only
NormalConfiguration change in productionTech Lead + weekly change board
EmergencyFix during a P1 incidentTech Lead by phone, board reviews after

Raising a normal change

  1. Create the change record: what, why, when, and who is affected.
  2. Add a test result and a rollback plan. A change without a rollback plan is rejected.
  3. Submit by Tuesday 12:00 for Thursday's change board.
  4. After the change, confirm it worked and close the record within one day.

Freeze periods

No normal changes in the last three working days of the month (client reporting). Emergency changes only.

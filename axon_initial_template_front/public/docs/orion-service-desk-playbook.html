Orion service desk playbook

Project Orion (ORI-330) · Owner: Ada Quinn, Tech Lead · Folder: /Orion/ServiceDesk/KT · Verified 20 Sep 2026

1. Purpose

How a Service Desk Analyst on Orion handles requests and incidents for the client, from the first ticket to working independently. Use it with your KT plan; your Tech Lead confirms each level.

2. Standard service requests

  1. Pick the oldest unassigned ticket in the ORI-Requests queue.
  2. Check the requester and the catalogue item. Password resets, access to shared folders and software installs follow the catalogue steps.
  3. Write what you did in the ticket in plain words, then resolve it. The requester gets an automatic note.
  4. If the catalogue has no step for it, ask in the team channel before you improvise.

3. Incident triage

  • P1: several users or a whole service down. Start the Major incident procedure at once.
  • P2: one service degraded for a team. Assign to second line within 30 minutes.
  • P3/P4: one user, a workaround exists. Handle in the queue.

4. Complex scenarios (covered in KT with your Tech Lead)

ScenarioWhat you practise
Multi-system outageLink related tickets, keep one master incident, update the status page every 30 minutes.
Bulk data correctionUse the approved script only, get a second pair of eyes, log every changed record.

5. Reporting and shift handover

Before you leave, post the open P1/P2 list and anything waiting on a third party in the handover note. The next shift reads it first.

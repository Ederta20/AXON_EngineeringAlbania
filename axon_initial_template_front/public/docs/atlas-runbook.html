Atlas runbook

Project Atlas (ATL-512) · Owner: Ben Royce, Tech Lead and KT owner · Folder: /Team/Operations/Onboarding · Verified 11 Oct 2026

Daily checks (start of shift)

  1. Open the operations dashboard and confirm all overnight batch jobs show Completed.
  2. Check the alert inbox. Acknowledge each alert and note the action you took.
  3. Confirm the backup report for the previous day is green.

Access you need

Operations dashboard, scheduler (read and run), alert inbox. Request them through Access Support on your first day; your Line Manager approves.

End of shift

Write the handover note: alerts still open, jobs re-run, anything waiting on another team.

Major incident procedure

Project Orion (ORI-330) · Owner: Ada Quinn, Tech Lead · Folder: /Orion/ServiceDesk/Incidents · Not yet verified

When to use it

A P1 incident: several users or a whole service cannot work, or client data may be wrong or exposed.

First 15 minutes

  1. Open one master incident and link every related ticket to it.
  2. Call the on-duty Tech Lead. Do not wait for an email reply.
  3. Post a first status update: what is affected, since when, next update time.

Roles during the incident

RoleDoes
Incident lead (Tech Lead)Decides on fixes and escalations.
Communicator (Service Desk)Status updates every 30 minutes, answers users.
Resolver teamsInvestigate and fix, report back to the lead.

Closing

  • Confirm with two affected users that the service works.
  • Write a short summary: cause, fix, time to restore.
  • Book a review within 5 working days and add actions to the backlog.
